# Code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, respectful and safe project environment.

Participants must communicate constructively, respect privacy, avoid harassment and discrimination, and never pressure others to disclose credentials, personal data or private infrastructure details.

Security concerns must be handled responsibly. Do not publish exploit details that place users at immediate risk, access systems without permission or use the project to control devices you do not own or administer with explicit authorisation.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, misleading or privacy-violating contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and users.
